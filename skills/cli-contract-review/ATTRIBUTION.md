repository. This rewrite adds exact process identity, shell/TEMP matrices, durable
receipts, measured cold-start, rollback, stage authority, and endpoint continuity. See
the repository `UPSTREAM.md`.

and repeated-authorization contract gaps. The public companion is
[`bidking-inference`](https://github.com/SeasonCake/bidking-inference).
